Initial release of Podcast Merlin (v0.1.0):
- Bi-directional Nextcloud and gPodder sync
- Interactive Up Next playback queue with drag-and-drop reordering
- Resilient episode download manager with storage quota controls
- Configurable sleep timer with gentle audio fade-out
- Variable playback speed (0.5x–3.0x) and pitch normalization
- Android home screen player widget with theme sync
- Bluetooth audio focus recovery and smart resume
- OPML import and export support
